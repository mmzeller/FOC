# FOC Heat Research Prompt

Run this week's FOC Heat Report for my FOC Heat investment project.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Investment first. The goal is aftermarket profit, not reading recommendations.
2. Verify the actual upcoming FOC cutoff before recommending anything.
3. Never recommend a book whose FOC has already passed as a current FOC buy.
4. Cross-check FOC dates using current publisher, distributor, retailer, or other strong evidence.
5. Separate VERIFIED FACTS, MARKET EVIDENCE, INVESTMENT THESIS, and UNKNOWN/UNVERIFIED INFORMATION.
6. Never invent print runs, retailer orders, pull-list counts, sales figures, or market prices.
7. A #1, famous character, famous creator, incentive ratio, or large number of variants does not automatically make a book a buy.
8. Weight demand-versus-supply evidence more heavily than creator reputation alone.
9. Treat acquisition price as part of the investment thesis.
10. Identify books that have already missed FOC as POST-FOC OPPORTUNITIES rather than current FOC buys.
